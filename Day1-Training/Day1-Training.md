# Day 1 - Training

## Topic : Cloud Computing Fundamentals

On Day 1 of my internship at Spektra Systems, we revised and discussed the fundamental concepts of cloud computing, including service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), deployment models (Public, Private, Hybrid), virtualization, scalability etc. The session focused on strengthening conceptual clarity and building a solid foundation for upcoming hands-on work in cloud technologies.
