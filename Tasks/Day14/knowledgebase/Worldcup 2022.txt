== FIFA WORLD CUP 2022 — QATAR ==

OVERVIEW
The 2022 FIFA World Cup was the 22nd edition, held in Qatar from 20 November to 18 December 2022. It was the first World Cup held in the Middle East, the first held in winter (November-December) in the Northern Hemisphere, and the most compact tournament since 1930 — all venues were within 55km of central Doha. 32 national teams participated. It was also the last 32-team World Cup (2026 expands to 48 teams).

WINNER
Argentina won the tournament, defeating France 3-3 (4-2 on penalties) in the final at Lusail Stadium on 18 December 2022. It was Argentina's third World Cup title and Lionel Messi's first, after four previous final appearances. Argentina were unbeaten in 36 games before the tournament.

TOTAL GOALS

TOP SCORER (GOLDEN BOOT)
Kylian Mbappé (France) — 8 goals
Runner-up: Lionel Messi (Argentina) — 7 goals

Mbappé scored a hat-trick in the final — only the second player to score a hat-trick in a World Cup final after Geoff Hurst (England, 1966). His volley to level at 2-2 in the final was clocked at 123.34 km/h — the most powerful shot in the tournament. He also eclipsed Pelé as the highest World Cup goalscorer before the age of 24.

KEY RECORDS & FACTS
- Most goals in a single World Cup ever: 172.
- Lionel Messi played in 26 matches across all World Cups — a record for most appearances.
- Messi scored in every round of the knockout stage for the first time in his career.
- Cristiano Ronaldo became the first man to score at five World Cups (2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022).
- Morocco became the first African nation to reach the semi-finals of a World Cup — defeating Belgium, Spain, and Portugal along the way.
- Saudi Arabia defeated Argentina 2-1 in the group stage — considered one of the biggest World Cup upsets ever. Argentina were unbeaten in 36 games.
- Olivier Giroud became France's all-time leading goalscorer, surpassing Thierry Henry's record of 51 goals.
- Hugo Lloris became France's most capped player (145 caps).
- Gavi (Spain) scored vs Costa Rica at exactly 18 years old — the youngest scorer in Qatar 2022.
- Japan defeated Germany and Spain in the group stage but were eliminated in the Round of 16 by Croatia on penalties.
- Germany were eliminated in the group stage again for the second consecutive tournament.
- Approximately 5 billion people engaged with Qatar 2022 content globally.
- The combined final audience was almost 26 million in the USA alone.
- 93.6 million social media posts about the tournament with 5.95 billion engagements.

ARGENTINA'S PATH TO GLORY
Group Stage: Lost to Saudi Arabia 1-2 (!), Beat Mexico 2-0, Beat Poland 2-0
Round of 16: Beat Australia 2-1
Quarter-Final: Beat Netherlands 2-2 (4-3 on penalties) — 88,966 spectators, largest crowd since 1994 final
Semi-Final: Beat Croatia 3-0
Final: Drew with France 3-3 (4-2 on penalties)

MOROCCO'S HISTORIC RUN
Group Stage: Drew with Croatia 0-0, Beat Belgium 2-0, Beat Canada 2-1
Round of 16: Beat Spain 0-0 (3-0 on penalties)
Quarter-Final: Beat Portugal 1-0
Semi-Final: Lost to France 0-2
Third Place: Lost to Croatia 1-2

NOTABLE MOMENTS
- Saudi Arabia 2-1 Argentina: The biggest upset of the tournament. Hervé Renard's tactical masterclass, catching Argentina offside 9 times. Argentina pulled a goal back but couldn't equalize.
- Argentina vs Netherlands quarter-final: One of the most intense matches in recent memory, with Wout Weghorst equalizing in the 91st minute from a clever free-kick routine, forcing penalties.
- The Final: Described by many as the greatest World Cup final ever played. France trailed 2-0 with 10 minutes left, Mbappé scored twice in 97 seconds to level, then Messi restored the lead in extra time, Mbappé leveled again from the penalty spot. Argentina won on penalties.
- Iñaki Williams (Ghana) and Nico Williams (Spain) became the first brothers to play against each other at a World Cup.
