== FIFA WORLD CUP 2014 — BRAZIL ==

OVERVIEW
The 2014 FIFA World Cup was the 20th edition, held in Brazil from 12 June to 13 July 2014 across 12 host cities. It was the second time Brazil hosted the tournament (first being 1950). 32 national teams participated. Bosnia and Herzegovina made their debut appearance.

WINNER
Germany won the tournament, defeating Argentina 1-0 in the final at the Maracanã Stadium, Rio de Janeiro, on 13 July 2014. Mario Götze scored the winning goal in the 113th minute of extra time with a left-foot volley. It was Germany's 4th World Cup title and the first for a reunified Germany. They became the first European team to win a World Cup staged in the Americas.

FINAL RESULT
Germany 1-0 Argentina (after extra time)
Goal: Mario Götze (113')
Venue: Maracanã Stadium, Rio de Janeiro

THIRD PLACE
Netherlands 3-0 Brazil

SEMI-FINALS
Germany 7-1 Brazil (the infamous "Mineirazo" / "Miracle of Bern" humiliation of hosts)
Argentina 0-0 Netherlands (Argentina won 4-2 on penalties)

TOTAL GOALS
171 goals in 64 matches (average 2.67 per match) — tied the all-time World Cup record at the time.

TOP SCORER (GOLDEN BOOT)
James Rodríguez (Colombia) — 6 goals in 5 matches
Runner-up: Thomas Müller (Germany) — 5 goals, 3 assists
James also won the Puskás Award for his stunning left-foot volley against Uruguay, voted goal of the tournament.

OTHER AWARDS
Golden Ball (Best Player): Lionel Messi (Argentina)
Silver Ball: Thomas Müller (Germany)
Bronze Ball: Arjen Robben (Netherlands)
Golden Glove (Best Goalkeeper): Manuel Neuer (Germany)
Best Young Player: Paul Pogba (France)

KEY RECORDS & FACTS
- Miroslav Klose (Germany) scored his 16th World Cup goal against Brazil, becoming the all-time World Cup top scorer, surpassing Ronaldo's record of 15.
- Klose became the first player to appear in four World Cup semi-finals.
- Goal-line technology was used at a World Cup finals for the first time.
- Vanishing spray for free kicks was also introduced for the first time.
- Spain, reigning world champions, crashed out in the group stage — the end of the "tiki-taka" era.
- Brazil conceded 14 goals in the tournament — the most by a host nation in history.
- The 7-1 defeat of Brazil by Germany is considered the greatest upset in World Cup history for a host nation.
- James Rodríguez was 23 years old when he won the Golden Boot, announcing himself to the world.
- Neymar (Brazil) scored 4 goals before being injured in the quarter-finals.

GERMANY'S PATH TO GLORY
Group Stage: Beat Portugal 4-0, drew with Ghana 2-2, beat USA 1-0
Round of 16: Beat Algeria 2-1 (AET)
Quarter-Final: Beat France 1-0
Semi-Final: Beat Brazil 7-1
Final: Beat Argentina 1-0 (AET)

NOTABLE MOMENTS
- USA's Tim Howard made 16 saves against Belgium — a World Cup record for a single match.
- Costa Rica reached the quarter-finals as massive underdogs, finishing ahead of Uruguay, Italy, and England in their group.
- Algeria reached the Round of 16 for the first time, pushing Germany to extra time.
- The "Mineirazo": Germany's 7-1 demolition of Brazil in Belo Horizonte became an instant piece of football history. Goals from Müller (×4), Klose, Kroos (×2), and Khedira.
