== FIFA WORLD CUP 2018 — RUSSIA ==

OVERVIEW
The 2018 FIFA World Cup was the 21st edition, held in Russia from 14 June to 15 July 2018 across 11 host cities. It was the first World Cup held in Eastern Europe and the first to use VAR (Video Assistant Referee) technology. 32 national teams participated.

WINNER
France won the tournament, defeating Croatia 4-2 in the final at Luzhniki Stadium, Moscow, on 15 July 2018. It was France's second World Cup title (after 1998). The fourth consecutive World Cup title won by a European team (Italy 2006, Spain 2010, Germany 2014, France 2018).

VAR — FIRST USE AT A WORLD CUP
VAR made its World Cup debut in Russia. Diego Costa's goal against Portugal on 15 June 2018 was the first World Cup goal based on a VAR decision. The first VAR penalty was awarded to France against Australia, converted by Griezmann. A record number of penalties were awarded in the tournament, partly attributed to VAR.

KEY RECORDS & FACTS
- 12 own goals — a World Cup record.
- Record number of penalties awarded in a single tournament.
- Croatia reached their first-ever World Cup final.
- Luka Modrić won the Golden Ball, breaking the Messi/Ronaldo stranglehold on individual awards.
- Kylian Mbappé scored 4 goals at 19 years old, becoming the second teenager to score in a World Cup final (after Pelé in 1958).
- Cristiano Ronaldo scored a hat-trick against Spain — including a stunning free kick in the 88th minute to level at 3-3. Portugal drew 3-3 with Spain.
- Russia, the host nation, were eliminated in the quarter-finals by Croatia on penalties.
- Germany, the defending champions, were eliminated in the group stage — crashing out bottom of Group F despite beating South Korea 2-0 in the final group game (which wasn't enough).
- Iceland made their World Cup debut, becoming the smallest nation ever to qualify. They famously held Argentina to a 1-1 draw.
- Mohamed Salah scored for Egypt from the penalty spot — Egypt's first World Cup goal since 1990.
- England reached the semi-finals for the first time since 1990, defeating Colombia on penalties in the Round of 16.
- Japan were eliminated in the Round of 16 by Belgium despite leading 2-0, eventually losing 3-2 in one of the tournament's greatest comebacks.

NOTABLE MATCHES
- Portugal 3-3 Spain (Group stage): One of the greatest group games ever, featuring Ronaldo's hat-trick.
- Germany 0-2 South Korea: Germany eliminated from group stage as defending champions.
- Belgium 3-2 Japan: Belgium overturned a 2-0 deficit to win in the 94th minute.
- Russia 2-2 Spain (4-3 pens): Russia knocked out Spain, the 2010 world champions.
- Croatia 3-2 Argentina: Croatia destroyed Argentina in one of Modrić's finest performances.

FRANCE'S PATH TO GLORY
Group Stage: Beat Australia 2-1, Drew with Peru 0-0... Wait — Beat Peru 1-0, Drew with Denmark 0-0
Round of 16: Beat Argentina 4-3 (Mbappé scored twice, Di María scored a wonder goal)
Quarter-Final: Beat Uruguay 2-0
Semi-Final: Beat Belgium 1-0
Final: Beat Croatia 4-2

MBAPPÉ VS ARGENTINA — ROUND OF 16
France 4-3 Argentina was arguably the match of the tournament. Mbappé scored twice in 4 minutes and ran at Argentina's defence with devastating speed. Di María scored a stunning long-range opener. A classic match.
